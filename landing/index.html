Questions

What people ask us at demos

Something else you want to know about Solaris, the device or Mira? Write to the team.

Email the team

Which inverters does Solaris work with?

Hybrid inverters with an RS485 Modbus port. A new brand needs its register map added first.

Does Solaris need the internet?

No. Everything runs on your home network. Offline, the solar forecast uses your last 14 days in place of the weather.

How does Solaris forecast the bill?

From your daily grid use so far, at your Meralco rate: ₱15.06 per kWh unless you set another.

Does Mira make up numbers?

No. She answers from your own readings, and tests check she adds no new figures.

What happens during a brownout?

Solaris alerts your phone, and the Brownout Advisor shows how long the battery lasts at your current load, with Mira’s advice.

Can I buy a Solaris today?

Not yet. It is a working prototype by Team Visionaries of PLV, built for the IDOL Hackathon 2026.

How it connects

Wires into your inverter’s RS485 port

A small Solaris device reads the hybrid inverter over Modbus and sends each reading over Wi-Fi to a laptop at home.

  • Device: an ESP32 with an RS485 adapter
  • Server: FastAPI and SQLite on your laptop
  • Mira: Qwen 3.5 on Ollama, no cloud account
